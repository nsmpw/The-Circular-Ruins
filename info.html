Dear Visitor,

Welcome to the ruins.

What you are about to enter is an interactive adaptation of

The Circular Ruins (1940) by Jorge Luis Borges...

The story follows a man who arrives at a ruined temple with

one purpose: to dream another person into existence.

He believes he is the creator.

He believes he is in control.

But the longer he dreams, the less certain his position becomes.

If someone can be dreamed into existence, what makes the dreamer any different?
